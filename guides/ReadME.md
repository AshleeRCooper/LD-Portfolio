# Manager & Employee Guides

A collection of practical guides designed to support managers and employees 
through key moments in the employee lifecycle.

---

## Guides

### 🤝 Summer Training Facilitator Guide
**Collaborators:** Ashlee + Team  

A facilitator guide for managers leading team training during the summer period. 
Covers session structure, facilitation tips, and key talking points to ensure 
consistent delivery across teams.

---

### 📋 Intrayear Hire Guide
**Author:** Ashlee 

A step-by-step guide for managers welcoming new team members who join outside 
of the standard hiring cycle. Covers onboarding milestones, check-in cadences, 
and tips for setting new hires up for success.

---

### 📊 Quarterly Check-In Guide
**Author:** Ashlee

A multimedia guide walking employees and managers through completing their 
quarterly review. Includes:
- Step-by-step written instructions
- Walkthrough videos
- FAQs for common questions
